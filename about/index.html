About Pulse Point

Built from the trades. Built to challenge the agency playbook.

The founder

Built by someone who understands the trades from the inside.

Pulse Point Marketing was founded by Joe Rodriguez. Before any of this, Joe served as a Sergeant in the United States Marine Corps, then spent more than twenty years leading crews in commercial construction as a drywall foreman. Coordinating manpower, holding a job to a standard, solving problems on site when there was no time to waste.

That background is the whole point. Joe has run the kind of business you run. He knows the pressure of needing steady work, the frustration of doing great work that nobody sees, and the difference between talk and follow-through. He brings the same standards he learned in the Marines and on the jobsite to the way your reputation gets handled online.

You will not get a cookie-cutter plan, a junior account manager, or corporate buzzwords. Because Pulse Point is deliberately small and founder-led, Joe works on your business directly.
