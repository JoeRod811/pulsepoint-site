Why it matters

Reputation starts working before you ever answer the phone.

When two businesses sit next to each other on Google, the one with more recent, higher-rated reviews wins, almost every time. Customers trust reviews nearly as much as a recommendation from a friend, and they read them before they ever visit your website or pick up the phone.

The businesses pulling ahead did not get lucky. They built a habit of asking customers consistently, at a natural point after the job, with a process so simple it actually gets done. We install that habit for you, and we make it run off the tools you already use, like Jobber, so it fits your workflow instead of adding to it.

The process

Make the honest ask easy, consistent and fair.

A strong review system does not cherry-pick who gets asked. It gives eligible customers the same simple opportunity to share an honest review, then gives you a process for listening and responding well.

After a job wraps, eligible customers receive the same straightforward request for an honest Google review. Positive, neutral or critical, the opportunity to review stays the same. If a customer also needs help with a problem, your normal customer-service process gives them a direct way to reach you so you can address it.

Simple review request

One tap takes every eligible customer straight to the same Google review opportunity. Less friction means more customers actually finish what they intended to do.

Direct customer support

Customers who need help can still reach you directly through your normal support process. That gives you a chance to solve the problem without taking away their opportunity to leave an honest review.

The goal is not to control what customers say. It is to build a steady flow of real feedback and respond well when something goes wrong. Honest critical reviews are part of a believable reputation, and a thoughtful response can show future customers how you handle problems.
